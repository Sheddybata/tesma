Admissions

Ask about a place.

TESMA admits into crèche, day care, Kindergarten 1, Kindergarten 2, and Grades 1 to 5. Music and creative arts are part of the school, not a separate application. Admissions confirms which classes are taking pupils this term.

  1. 01

    Tell us the class

    Share your child’s age and the class you are asking about. If you are not sure whether that is day care, kindergarten, or a grade, say so.

  2. 02

    Call, or send a note

    Phone, WhatsApp, or the form. The note is prepared for +234 903 983 0321. Nothing is stored on this website.

  3. 03

    Visit on a school day

    Come to Rayfield–Old Airport Road, opposite MOPOL 8 Command, Monday to Friday, between 7:10am and 3:30pm. Call ahead on a public holiday.

Classes you can ask for

  • Crèche — the youngest children
  • Day care — care through the school day
  • Kindergarten 1 and 2 — the nursery years
  • Grades 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5

Have this ready

  • Your child’s age
  • The class you want, if you know it
  • A phone number we can reach you on
  • Any question about a scholarship place
  • The last school report, if the child is joining a grade from another school

Scholarships

Ask what is open. Do not guess.

At inauguration in September 2022, the board said 30 percent of pupils would be on scholarship, aimed at families of low income. That was the commitment. It is not an automatic place.

When you enquire, say that you are asking about a scholarship. Admissions will tell you what is available for the coming term and which papers a family should bring.

30%

Stated at inauguration. Current places are confirmed by the office, not by this page.

Questions

Before you visit.

Is there a Grade 6?

No. Primary at TESMA runs from Grade 1 through Grade 5.

Is nursery the same as kindergarten?

Yes, on this site. The classes are Kindergarten 1 and Kindergarten 2.

What time can I come?

Monday to Friday, 7:10am to 3:30pm. The academy is closed on Saturday and Sunday.

Where do I send the enquiry?

The form opens a WhatsApp chat with +234 903 983 0321. You can also call that number.
