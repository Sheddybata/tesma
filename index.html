Purpose

Built so a child is not left behind.

The academy belongs to Tanjuriel El-Buba Cooperation. It is led by Proprietor and Executive Director Mrs Nissi Xavier. It opened in Jos in September 2022 so children from different kinds of homes could have the same chance at a serious education.

  • Academic work

    A full school day and teaching that moves a child ready into the next class.

  • Character

    Spiritual discipline and conduct, so ability is not separated from how a child treats other people.

  • Creative skill

    Music and creative arts from the early years, taught as skill and not as decoration.

TESMA

Scholarships

The same classrooms, more than one kind of family.

TESMA was opened so children from low-income homes and children from better-off homes could be taught together. Scholarship programmes were part of that promise from the first day.

30%

At inauguration, the board said 30 percent of pupils would be on scholarship, with support aimed at families of low income.

That was the commitment made in September 2022. Places are limited. Admissions will say what is open for the coming term, and what a family needs to show.

Questions

Before you visit.

Which classes can I apply for?

Crèche, day care, Kindergarten 1 and 2, and Grades 1 to 5. Music and creative arts are part of the school, not a separate application. Ask admissions which classes are open this term.

How do scholarships work?

The board committed, at inauguration, to scholarship programmes for low-income families, and said 30 percent of pupils would be supported. Ask admissions what is available now and which papers a family should bring.

What time is the school day?

Monday to Friday, 7:10am to 3:30pm. The academy is closed on Saturday and Sunday. Call ahead on public holidays.

Where is TESMA?

Rayfield–Old Airport Road, opposite MOPOL 8 Command, Jos, Plateau State. The phone is +234 903 983 0321.
